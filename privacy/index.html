Privacy policy

Effective November 2026

Lumadose is a log for your GLP-1 medication, food, weight and how you feel. It was built so that this information stays yours. The short version: we don't collect any of it. There is no Lumadose account and no Lumadose server, and the app has no ads, analytics or tracking.

What you enter, and where it's kept

Everything you log stays on your iPhone:

  • your medications, the doses and schedules you enter, injection sites and reactions;
  • food, protein, fiber and water;
  • weight, waist, body fat and progress photos;
  • daily check-ins (appetite, side effects, mood) and notes;
  • your name, height, goals and settings.

If iCloud is on for your Apple Account, Lumadose keeps a copy of what you log in your own private iCloud database, so it's on your other devices and comes back if you replace your iPhone. Apple stores that data for you; we can't see it. You can turn this off in the Settings app under your name → iCloud.

Apple Health

Lumadose only uses Apple Health if you connect it. iOS asks you for each kind of data, and you can change your answers at any time in the Health app.

  • Saved to Health: the weight, waist, protein, fiber, water and mood you log, while "Save to Apple Health" is on.
  • Read from Health: weight and body fat from other apps and scales, so your charts are complete. Lumadose keeps these copies on your iPhone only. They never go to iCloud, and each of your devices reads them from Health itself.

Doses and progress photos are never saved to Health. Data from Health is never used for advertising and is never shared with anyone.

Barcode lookups

When you scan a barcode, Lumadose sends the barcode number, and nothing else, to Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org), a free public food database, to look up the product's protein and fiber. Nothing about you or your health goes with it. Open Food Facts' own privacy policy covers how it handles requests.

On-device features

Typed meals ("2 eggs and yogurt") are worked out by Apple's on-device language model, and meal photos by Apple's on-device image recognition. Neither sends your text or photos anywhere.

Purchases

Lumadose Plus is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment and tells the app only whether your subscription is active. We never see your name, email address or payment details.

Reminders and widgets

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Widgets and the pill timer read a summary the app keeps on your iPhone. None of them use a server.

Your report and exports

The doctor report (a PDF) and "Export your data" (spreadsheet files) are made on your iPhone. They go only where you choose to share or save them.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes everything Lumadose has stored on your iPhone and in your iCloud. Deleting the app removes it from your iPhone; to remove the iCloud copy too, use Delete all data first, or go to the Settings app → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. Data already saved to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app.

Children

Lumadose is not meant for children under 13.

Medical information

Lumadose is a logging tool, not a medical device. It doesn't give medical advice or suggest doses. Talk to your prescriber about your treatment.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because we don't collect your data, a change can't apply to data we already hold: there isn't any.
