Reminders

I'm not getting reminders

Check that notifications are allowed for Lumadose in your iPhone's Settings › Notifications. Then, in Lumadose, open Settings › Reminders: each medication has its own reminder switch and time. A paused medication doesn't send reminders.

Can I log a dose without opening the app?

Yes. Touch and hold the reminder (or swipe down on it) for Log now, Snooze 1 hour and Skip this dose. The next-dose widget can log a dose that's due, too.

Doses and medications

I missed a dose

Today shows it as not logged. If you took it, tap I took it; if not, tap Mark as skipped. Lumadose doesn't move your schedule and can't tell you what to do after a missed dose: check your medication's label or ask your prescriber.

My prescriber changed my dose

Open Meds, tap the medication, then Change dose, and enter the new dose exactly as your prescriber wrote it. Earlier doses stay in its history and on your charts.

How do I pause or stop a medication?

Open Meds, tap the medication, then Pause or stop. Pausing keeps it on Today without reminders. Stopping moves it to past medications. Either way, every log stays.

How does the site suggestion work?

Lumadose suggests the injection site you've gone longest without, and never the one you used last time. You can tap any other site instead. It's a reminder to rotate, not medical guidance.

Food

A barcode isn't found

Barcodes are looked up in Open Food Facts, a free database that anyone can add to, so some products are missing. Enter the protein from the pack instead, and save it to your favourites so it's one tap next time. If the camera won't read a barcode, type the digits printed under it.

I can't type a meal in words

Typed meals use Apple's on-device model, so they need an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with it turned on in Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri. On other iPhones you can still search, scan and use favourites.

Where does the protein goal come from?

When you set a goal, Lumadose shows general guidance of 1.2 to 1.6 g of protein per kg of goal weight a day. It's a starting point, not advice for you: set whatever goal your care team gives you, in Settings › Daily goals.

Apple Health

What does Lumadose share with Health?

While Save to Apple Health is on, Lumadose saves the weight, waist, protein, fiber, water and mood you log. It reads weight and body fat from other apps and scales, and keeps those on your iPhone only. Doses and photos are never saved to Health. Change what's allowed in the Health app: tap your picture, then Apps, then Lumadose.

My scale's weights don't show up

Open Settings › Apple Health in Lumadose and tap Connect, then allow Lumadose to read weight and body fat. New readings come in each time you open the app.

iCloud and a new iPhone

How do I move my log to a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account with iCloud on, and install Lumadose. Your log comes back from iCloud on its own. Weights from other apps are read from Apple Health again.

Is my log syncing?

Settings › iCloud sync in Lumadose shows whether sync is on and up to date. If it says "Not signed in", sign in to iCloud in your iPhone's Settings.

Lumadose Plus

What's free, and what does Plus add?

Reminders and logging are always free: one medication with every dose and reminder, food, water, weight and check-ins, and charts for the last 30 days. Plus adds more medications, barcode scanning, typed and photo meals, charts past 30 days and side effects by day after your dose, the doctor report, and more widgets.

How do the free trial and cancelling work?

The yearly plan starts with 7 days free. You can cancel any time in your iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends. If Plus ends, nothing is deleted: every medication keeps its reminders and history.

I bought Plus but it isn't showing

In Lumadose, open Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Settings › Export your data makes a zip of spreadsheet (CSV) files: doses, body measurements, food, water and check-ins, in your units.

How do I delete everything?

Settings › Delete all data removes your log from your iPhone and from your iCloud. Data already saved to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app. See the privacy policy for more.

Lumadose is a logging tool, not a medical device. It doesn't give medical advice or suggest doses. Talk to your prescriber about your treatment.
